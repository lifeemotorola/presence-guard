KEYBLOOM 1.3 — WINDOWS QUICK START
By Emmanuel Suah

REQUIREMENTS: 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11, a physical keyboard, and enough
space for the extracted app. No compiler, Node.js, account, or subscription is
needed. The app works offline after download.

1. Download keybloom-windows-x64 from the KeyBloom GitHub Actions run.
2. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All. Choose a folder you
   can keep, such as Documents\KeyBloom. Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click KeyBloom.exe.
   KEEP ALL THE OTHER FILES AND FOLDERS BESIDE THE EXE: the executable is not a
   single-file app. To move it, move the whole extracted folder.
4. Choose "Start your first lesson". Type into the box, following the text and
   the highlighted key. The timer begins with your first keystroke. Correct
   errors with Backspace; corrected errors still count in accuracy.
5. Open Lessons for all 500 practices, including the original 21. Search by
   title, topic, key, or number; use the topic/progress filters and page controls
   to browse. Each lesson may be repeated.
6. In Practice, choose Story typing for one of 80 randomly chosen stories. Each
   has at least 700 words. Choose Another story for a different one, or Type
   this story to begin. Stories are historical texts; see STORY-CREDITS.txt.
7. In Practice, choose Import a text file to select a local .txt file. It must
   be UTF-8, at most 256 KiB (262,144 bytes) and 10–80,000 characters after
   formatting. Extra spaces and line breaks become spaces; common smart quotes,
   dashes and accents are simplified. Other scripts and emoji are not supported.
   The original file is not changed. Story and file practice are untimed: pause
   as needed, or choose Finish early after typing to save a summary.
8. Practice also includes timed tests, Word Sprint, a focused letter drill, and
   Practice your own text (10–500 printable US keyboard characters).
9. Open My progress to see recent results, letters to practice and milestones.
   In Settings you can choose a larger practice text size, change the daily goal,
   show or hide the keyboard, or reset your locally saved progress.

UPDATING FROM KEYBLOOM 1.0, 1.1 OR 1.2
Close the old app, extract this version into a new folder, and open its
KeyBloom.exe. Progress stays in your Windows profile; do not delete
%APPDATA%\KeyBloom if you want to keep it. The original 21 lessons are unchanged.
From 1.0 or 1.1, completions still in the old recent history are imported;
completions already lost from that 500-session history cannot be recovered.
Sessions made in 1.0 did not record letter counts. After upgrading, use 1.3 or
newer: older versions can discard story and file summaries, and versions before
1.2 can also erase the separate completion list. There is no automatic update
or cloud backup.

WINDOWS SECURITY
This build is not digitally signed. Windows may show a publisher/reputation
warning. Only run a download you trust; compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt
and the checksum published on the matching Actions run. A matching checksum
identifies the file; it does not prove the app is safe. Do not turn off Windows
security globally.

PRIVACY AND LIMITS
- Practiced lesson IDs, up to 500 recent session summaries, and settings are
  saved in your Windows user profile under %APPDATA%\KeyBloom. Completion IDs
  are kept separately, even when old session details roll off. Other statistics
  and most milestones reflect the retained 500 sessions. KeyBloom saves aggregate
  letter attempt and error counts for built-in exercises, not the words you type.
  KeyBloom does not save your passages, imported filenames or text, or their
  letter counts. Imported text stays in memory for retry and is cleared when you
  leave that practice. Windows may keep its own file-dialog history. Resetting
  or deleting the profile permanently removes progress. There is no cloud backup.
- Story texts are public domain in the United States; rules may differ elsewhere.
  Some contain dated perspectives or violence. See STORY-CREDITS.txt for sources.
- The course and visual keyboard use an English (US) QWERTY layout. Other
  physical layouts may not match the on-screen key hints.
- KeyBloom is an original, independent typing tutor; it is not Mavis Beacon
  and is not affiliated with its publisher. It does not reproduce every feature.
