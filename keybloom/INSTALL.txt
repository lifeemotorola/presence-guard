KEYBLOOM 1.0 — WINDOWS QUICK START
By Emmanuel Suah

REQUIREMENTS: 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11, a physical keyboard, and enough
space for the extracted app. No compiler, Node.js, account, or subscription is
needed. The app works offline after download.

1. Download keybloom-windows-x64 from the KeyBloom GitHub Actions run.
2. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All. Choose a folder you
   can keep, such as Documents\KeyBloom. Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click KeyBloom.exe.
   KEEP ALL THE OTHER FILES AND FOLDERS BESIDE THE EXE: the executable is not a
   single-file app. To move it, move the whole extracted folder.
4. Choose "Start your first lesson". Type into the box, following the text and
   the highlighted key. The timer begins with your first keystroke. Correct
   errors with Backspace; corrected errors still count in accuracy.
5. Open Lessons for the full course, Practice for a timed test or Word Sprint,
   and My progress for your history. Use Settings to change the daily goal,
   show or hide the keyboard, or reset your locally saved progress.

WINDOWS SECURITY
This build is not digitally signed. Windows may show a publisher/reputation
warning. Only run a download you trust; compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt
and the checksum published on the matching Actions run. A matching checksum
identifies the file; it does not prove the app is safe. Do not turn off Windows
security globally.

PRIVACY AND LIMITS
- Progress summaries and settings are saved in your Windows user profile under
  %APPDATA%\KeyBloom. The words you type are not saved. Resetting or deleting
  this data permanently removes the history; reinstalling can also lose it if
  you remove the profile. There is no account or cloud backup.
- The course and visual keyboard use an English (US) QWERTY layout. Other
  physical layouts may not match the on-screen key hints.
- KeyBloom is an original, independent typing tutor; it is not Mavis Beacon
  and is not affiliated with its publisher. It does not reproduce every feature.
